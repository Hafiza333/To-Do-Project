THE COST OF INACTION

Why Sticking to the Grid is Costing You Thousands

Traditional utility reliance leaves homeowners and business operators vulnerable to unpredictable, compounding systemic issues.

01
⚠
Skyrocketing Grid Tariffs
Electricity rates have surged globally by over 25% in the last 3 years. These operational overheads directly erode corporate profits and disposable home income.
02
⚠
Unstable Grid Infrastructure
Aging networks mean more frequent blackouts and brownouts. Without physical battery storage, a power failure freezes operations and compromises residential safety.
03
⚠
Dead Roof Assets
Hundreds of square meters of direct sunlight absorption are wasted every single day. Your unoccupied roof is an uncapitalized asset actively leaking potential revenue.
